# AirQuality-Tracer
a hardware project device that sense the air quality and show it in form or through led colors 

# what is it :
Ii is a simple hardware project that uses a MQ135 senser that sense the air quality of the environment and uses Arduino  to give that reading a saving point so it act like a brain based on the data the Arduino help displaying color to indicate the air quality.

#features:
directly connect to your laptop or anu devices with ports 
simple 3 minimal led 
keep you consious about your environmemt
portable
